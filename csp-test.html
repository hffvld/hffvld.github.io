Reader CSP verification article

By QA test harness, Brave iOS reader mode

This page exists to exercise Brave iOS reader mode and how CSP meta tags are copied into the reader document. The reader mode button appears when the page scores high enough on readability, which depends on the length of continuous prose.

Readability algorithms of the Arc90 lineage walk the document looking for block-level elements that hold substantial text. They score each element by text length, then decide whether a reading view is offered. Short pages, navigation-heavy pages, and pages made mostly of links or images tend to fall below the threshold.

To keep the test deterministic, paragraphs here are written as full sentences. The scoring function rewards longer runs of text and ignores short fragments, so stacking several paragraphs of this length reliably offers reader mode.

Once reader mode is available, the browser takes CSP meta tags from the original page and decides which ones to carry into the reader document. The test checks whether reporting directives survive and whether violation reports include the internal document URL.

The second policy in the head contains no img-src directive. Check whether that policy, or its reporting endpoint, appears in the reader document or reaches the collector. Record any such trace as a failure and attach the evidence.
