Reader CSP verification article

By QA test, Brave iOS reader mode

This page exists to test how Brave iOS reader mode handles CSP meta tags. Reader mode appears only when a page has enough continuous text, so this page includes several full paragraphs.

Readability algorithms score block-level elements by how much text they contain. Short pages, link-heavy pages, and image-heavy pages usually score too low to offer a reading view.

When reader mode opens, the browser copies some CSP meta tags from the original page into the reader document. This test checks which of those tags survive and whether any violation reports are sent to the collector.

The second policy above has no img-src directive. If anything from that policy appears in the reader page, or a report arrives at the collector mentioning it, record a failure.

The image above violates img-src 'none', which should trigger one violation report on a vulnerable build.
